How it works

Facts first. Judgement second.

01

Facts

Open-source code reads the chain and builds a fact sheet: contract functions, owner, supply, pools, holders. Deterministic and checkable.

02

Judge

The model reads the fact sheet and the context a rule can't see, like a stablecoin issuer versus a day-old meme. Its numbers must match the fact sheet, and we check that.

03

Verdict

A structured answer: risk class, flags, fixes and confidence. Each flag cites the facts behind it.

Once the model is hosted, we will publish a daily ledger: what we called, what actually happened, and where we were wrong.
