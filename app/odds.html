      The full table

      From Coroner's open collector (data/odds.json on GitHub), rebuilt every hour. A token is measured when our collector first sees its pool (a median 21 minutes after birth) and again 24 to 30 hours later. Still trading means at least $50 of volume or 3 swaps in the 24 hours before that re-check. Causes of death are patterns consistent with the numbers, never accusations. This is a record of what happened to past tokens, not a prediction for any one token and not financial advice.
